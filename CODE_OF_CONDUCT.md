# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
summarized here.

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith, give specific and kind feedback, accept
feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, discriminatory language,
publishing someone's private information, or anything else that would be inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and to anyone representing
the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block contributors who don't
follow this code. To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or contact the
maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately.
